> Torrents now show their share ratio, which survives restarts, and downloaded albums report their size.

## ✨ New

- **Downloads** — each torrent shows its share ratio; the cumulative upload is stored, so the ratio is kept across restarts and cleared when the torrent is removed.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Offline** — completed downloads report their size, so downloaded albums show it.
- **Library** — the offline badge on an album is aligned with the left edge of the cover.
