> Small polish for playing from search and the Home screen.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Home** — the album of the track that is playing now shows up in "Recently played" right away.
- **Search** — tracks and albums already in your library carry only the "in library" mark; the extra "Open" link is gone.
- **Library** — in the catalog the "in library" mark appears only on the album itself or on the track you added, without the "Open" link.
