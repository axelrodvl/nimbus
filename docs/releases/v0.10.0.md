> Search that keeps you informed and never gives up, and additions that show up in your library the moment you tap "Add".

## ✨ New

- **Library** — anything you add from search (artist, album or track) appears right away under "Recently added" on Home and at the top of the Library, marked "Adding…", and turns into the real card once the server has it.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Search** — while results load, the screen says what is happening ("Searching MusicBrainz…") and explains when MusicBrainz is slow, instead of showing only placeholder tiles.
- **Search** — when MusicBrainz times out or is unavailable, search no longer shows an error: it shows a "retrying" status and keeps trying on its own until results arrive, re-requesting only the sections that failed and keeping what already loaded.
- **Search** — the "Add" button is now a bright accent button and easy to spot.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Search** — results of the previous query no longer stay on screen while you type a new one.
