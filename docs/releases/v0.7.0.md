> A reliability release: background downloads, imports and searches now recover on their own instead of getting stuck.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Downloads** — dead or failed downloads retry on a growing schedule, switch to another source when one fails permanently, and stalled torrents with no peers are detected faster.
- **Downloads** — after an update or restart, stuck albums and lost jobs are picked up again automatically.
- **Backend** — workers, maintenance loops and shutdown are supervised: a crashing job no longer takes others down, crash-looping jobs are failed, and shutdown is bounded and parallel.
- **Backend** — the MusicBrainz request rate recovers by itself after throttling.
- **Search** — albums being downloaded keep their search schedule, and failed providers get a short retry.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Downloads** — album download progress no longer rolls back when a track finishes.
- **Downloads** — completed downloads are finalized atomically, so a crash can no longer leave a finished album half-imported.
- **Library** — play and download buttons are hidden for albums that were not found.
- **Docker** — Postgres runs under an init process, so orphaned health checks no longer trigger crash recovery.
