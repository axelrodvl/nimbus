> Play straight from search — browse artists and albums, tap a track to listen, with sturdier source handling behind it.

## ✨ New

- **Search** — browse an artist's discography and an album's tracklist right from search, and tap a track to play it without adding it to your library; an "Add to library" action is available from the player menu.
- **Search** — album results also include the top artist's discography, so albums are found even when MusicBrainz text search misses the artist's alias.
- **Sources** — choose one torrent for a whole album; available formats and the torrent in use are shown, and "Sources" and "Delete" are buttons on not-found albums.
- **Player** — a global indicator shows that a track is being prepared, with an explicit cancel; a track found outside the canonical release is added as a bonus track.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Search** — the search field has a clear button instead of an in-field spinner, the last query and scroll position are kept when you return to the Search tab, and the duplicate "looking for a source" toast is gone.
- **Player** — playback starts reliably from the tap on mobile, whole-album play starts from any playable track and queues the full tracklist, and waiting for a still-downloading CUE image no longer ends in a false "no peers" error.
- **Library** — MusicBrainz data and found torrents are never purged from the cache and stale data is served instantly; when space runs out, files not in your library are evicted first.
- **Library** — the track number no longer overlaps the play/equalizer overlay in the catalog.
- **Backend** — a warning is logged on every "no peers" timeout, and MusicBrainz requests for playback have a timeout with one retry.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Player** — a track no longer loses its position on seek while its image is still downloading.
- **Search** — played-track discovery gaps and a request race are fixed, and catalog library status and the playing indicator are correct.
