> Faster, steadier artwork and a round of mobile polish: covers are now cached on the server, and several iPhone and player glitches are gone.

## ✨ New

- **Library** — a step-by-step progress view on the artist page shows where an import stands, replacing the console-style log.
- **Backend** — album covers and artist photos are served from a disk cache at `/api/covers` with long-lived `Cache-Control: immutable` headers, so artwork loads once and stays fast.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Offline** — the service worker keeps covers and artist photos without evicting them, caches only successful responses, and loads third-party covers through CORS.
- **Settings** — font sizes are consistent across the screen: values, hints and version numbers now share one scale.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Player** — opening the "…" menu in the full-screen player no longer locks page scrolling on iOS, and swipes inside the menu no longer drag the player.
- **Player** — the bottom menu sheet no longer appears underneath the tab bar or the full-screen player.
- **Library** — the artist import status no longer overflows the screen on narrow displays.
- **Mobile** — toast notifications respect the safe area and no longer hide behind the iPhone notch.
